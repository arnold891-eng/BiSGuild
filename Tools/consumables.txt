# Why a file and not a rule in the code: the buff does NOT carry the item's name. "Flask of
# Chromatic Wonder" shows up as "Chromatic Wonder", and the Shattrath flasks as "... of Shattrath".
# A regex on "Flask of" misses half of them and silently reports those raiders as unprepared, which
# is the most confident kind of wrong. So the list is data, it is reviewed by someone who knows what
# the guild drinks, and `logreport.py --review` prints anything it has never been told about.
#
# Battle and guardian elixirs read identically in a log - the difference is in the item, not the
# aura - so the PAIR is counted: two elixirs up at once is one of each, because the game will not
# let two of a kind stack.
